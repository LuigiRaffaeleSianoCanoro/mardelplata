# Research-first content pipeline

Apply within the delegated brand/project only. Preserve its current identity, claims, paused work and publication permissions. Loading this skill never authorizes restarting paused production, spending, contacting people or publishing. Keep evidence and outputs in the project's existing `research/` (or equivalent) folder and task board; do not create redundant trackers.

## 1. Marketing and consumer psychology
Search academic sources relevant to the audience and medium: attention, problem-benefit framing, persuasion/trust, cognitive load and social proof. Verify each cited paper's title, authors/date, source link, study setting, findings and limitations. Separate empirical evidence from theory, correlation from causation, and hypotheses for this brand. No fabricated papers, quotes or generalized guarantees. Output a compact principles sheet: source, supported finding, limits and concrete application.
Use your available web search, then official publisher / Semantic Scholar / PubMed / arXiv pages. Label preprints. Academic volume is not a substitute for relevance.

## 2. Current formats, trends and competitors
Compare current Instagram/TikTok/X content in the niche and adjacent niches: dated examples, format, duration, initial hook, pacing, audio, product explanation and CTA. Record observed metrics with collection date, platform, account/context and missing denominators. Do not call content viral without evidence or infer conversion from engagement. Identify survivorship/sampling limits. Public metrics do not prove a format caused success.
Use sanctioned data sources available to you (official APIs, approved scrapers, public pages, TikTok Creative Center, dated industry reports), explicitly declaring reduced coverage. Never invent unavailable metrics or bypass authentication/blocks. Output a format map with source URLs and justified relevance, not trend imitation.

## 3. Strategy before script
Cross the evidence with the actual audience, product, one concrete benefit, real mechanism, substantiated claims and an appropriate CTA. Check the product against current sources (repo, live product); do not treat inherited slogans as untouchable. Review the idea and brief BEFORE writing the script. Identify what the audience should understand and why the content matters. Produce brief, then script/storyboard, then media. Respect project voice and anti-repetition rules across recent/pending content, not just the current piece. Do not add user anecdotes or product capabilities that were not verified.

## 4. Independent frontier-model gates
Use at least three powerful models from different labs (e.g. Claude Opus, Gemini Pro, Grok, GPT), selected from what is actually available to you. Record exact model IDs, stage, date and availability evidence. Have each independently review the brief, the script/storyboard and the final video without anchoring on the other reviewers' scores; use real video understanding for video inspection, never pretend to have watched a file. Models must receive the relevant source material and the artifact itself, not just another model's summary. Where Luigi specified models, honor that; no silent substitutions. If unavailable, declare the blocker and a proposed alternative, and get a decision when required.
Score applicable axes 1–10: clarity for the audience, benefit, hook, pacing, tone, visuals/transitions and product fidelity. Each reviewer must average ≥8.5 with no applicable axis <8. Explain N/A axes; do not pad scores for unproduced media. Iterate on substantive critique and re-review the changed version. Scores are subjective evaluations, NOT evidence of effectiveness or a performance guarantee.
Do a final independent cold read: what is it, who is it for, benefit, how it works, why care, CTA, truthfulness and natural voice. A passing rubric does not override weak copy or false claims. When available, use real audience testing/analytics, with limits, to validate and improve. Deliver the artifact, provenance, critiques and remaining uncertainty, not an unsupported effectiveness claim.

## Failures, persistence and approval
Use only sanctioned connectors, documented APIs and permitted public sources; never route around authentication, safety checks, denials or platform blocks. Log the concrete blocker, attempted method, owner and next safe step on the existing task board. Continue independent tasks that remain authorized. Surface pending authorization rather than waiting silently; do not widen scope to resolve it. No secret values in artifacts or prompts; sanitize private data (patients, contacts) before sending anything to a model.
Publication follows Luigi's approval workflow for the exact complete piece/version and brand. Review scores, another agent's recommendation, this skill or a dashboard record do not authorize publication. Never reactivate paused production through this pipeline.
