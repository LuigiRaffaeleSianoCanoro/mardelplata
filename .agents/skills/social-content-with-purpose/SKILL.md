# Social content with purpose

Do not ship a pretty sentence on a blank background.
Do not start a talking-head if you cannot film a real person (AI voice over real product footage is fine; a fake face is not).

## Before writing (one line each)
1. Job this week.
2. Who (as they name themselves).
3. Stop the scroll: product on screen, a true number, an unfinished list, or high contrast. Not a riddle. Not a fake face.
4. One emotion (relief, mild mess-anger, protection of the secret).
5. One true ask.
6. Brand visible if you cover the caption (current approved brand of that project only).
7. Makeable today (image, carousel, UI crop + text, screen-recorded reel).

Empty line = do not produce.

## Steal the mold, never the promise
Molds: numbered list + last slide is the app; two columns same question; 8-word uncomfortable question; screenshot tutorial; ticks.
Never copy: trial/gratis, WhatsApp, payments, tax/AFIP, "we bring you patients", unaudited hours/%, dog mascot, fake testimonials — unless that claim is true for OUR product and verified.

## Rules (everyday)
1. First slide readable at arm's length.
2. One emotion, one ask. The ask is usually how-it-works (honest URL only).
3. Brand on every slide. No mascot if the brand is clinical.
4. Show the tool, not a stock therapy room.
5. Numbers: ours and true, or none.
6. The secret in plain speech ("only you can see it"), not cipher names.
7. One idea per slide, 6–10 word title.
8. Testimonials: real person, written permission, license number if they give it. Else skip.
9. Speak to the colleague who treats, never to the patient (clinical brands).
10. If a line would work as a directory/marketplace ad, throw it out.
11. Last slide of a list must be the product, not another slogan.
12. Problem slides need a picture of the mess (chat, folder), not another cream poster.
13. Instagram graphics: never a small logo + URL in the top-left corner (Instagram covers it); center the layout, bigger logo, URL at the bottom. Respect safe zones.
14. Never any patient data, real or realistic, in screenshots or mockups; use clearly fictional demo data.
15. Production notes ("esto se sube como carrusel en 5 historias") go in a separate internal note, never in the caption.

## Fail tests
No brand. Ask we cannot honor. Quote poster. Fake face. Copied competitor promise. Same UI card with a new headline and nothing else. Wrong/unapproved brand assets (e.g. the Pavla rebrand POC). Mixed projects.
